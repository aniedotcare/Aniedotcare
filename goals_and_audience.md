# Goals and Audience

## Primary Goals
- Get bookings for doula services.
- Encourage users to book a free phone consultation.
- Generate leads through contact form submissions.

## Secondary Goals
- Collect email subscribers for a newsletter.
- Publish helpful resources for expectant parents.

## Target Audience
- Expectant parents in Brooklyn and surrounding NYC boroughs.

## Main Call to Action (CTA)
- "Book a Free Consult"
