# Post-Launch Growth Plan

Here are a few ideas for growing your online presence and attracting more clients after the website is launched.

## 1. Add a Simple Blog

**Frequency:** 1 post every 2–4 weeks.

**Topic Ideas:**
- Preparing for labor
- Postpartum tips
- Breathing techniques for labor
- Local resources in Brooklyn for new parents
- What to pack in your hospital bag
- The benefits of having a doula

A blog is a great way to share your expertise, attract new clients through search engines, and provide valuable content for your existing clients.

## 2. Email Capture & Nurturing

**Strategy:** Offer a valuable resource in exchange for an email address.

**Resource Idea:** A downloadable PDF, such as "5 Things to Pack for Labor" or a "Postpartum Recovery Checklist".

**Implementation:**
1.  Add a simple 1-field signup form to your website (e.g., in the footer or on the blog).
2.  Set up an email marketing service (like Mailchimp or ConvertKit) to automatically send the resource to new subscribers.
3.  Send a welcome email to new subscribers, introducing yourself and your services.

## 3. Gather and Showcase Testimonials

**Strategy:** Actively ask satisfied clients for reviews and testimonials.

**Implementation:**
1.  After working with a client, send them a follow-up email asking for a review. You can direct them to your Google Business Profile, Yelp page, or simply ask for a quote you can use on your website.
2.  Regularly update the "Testimonials" page on your website with new quotes.

## 4. Local SEO

**Strategy:** Improve your visibility in local search results.

**Implementation:**
1.  **Claim and optimize your Google Business Profile.** Make sure your name, address, and phone number (NAP) are consistent across all platforms.
2.  **Get listed on other relevant local directories,** such as Yelp.
3.  **Engage with local community parenting groups** on social media (e.g., Facebook groups). Share your blog posts and resources, and participate in conversations to build your reputation as a trusted local expert.
