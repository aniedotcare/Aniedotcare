Hi, I’m Anie Denaud

As a New York City-based doula, my passion is to provide unwavering emotional, physical, and informational support to growing families. I believe every birthing person deserves to feel safe, respected, and empowered.

With a calming presence and a deep well of knowledge in comfort measures, I'm here to support not just the birthing parent, but partners and family as well, ensuring everyone feels prepared and included.

My Services

Comprehensive support tailored to your unique needs.

🤰

Prenatal Support

3 prenatal visits to discuss your birth preferences, practice comfort techniques, and ensure you and your partner feel fully prepared.

🧘‍♀️

Labor & Birth

Continuous in-person support from the moment you need me in active labor, providing comfort, advocacy, and guidance.

👶

Postpartum

2-3 postpartum visits to process your birth, offer feeding support, and answer newborn care questions.

🌸

Encapsulation

Safe and professional placenta encapsulation services to support your postpartum recovery and well-being.
