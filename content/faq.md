# FAQ

**Q: What exactly does a doula do?**
A: I provide non-medical emotional, informational, and physical support before, during, and after birth.

**Q: Do you work with my provider?**
A: Yes — I collaborate with your OB/midwife and care team; I never replace medical advice.
