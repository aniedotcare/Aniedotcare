Hi, I’m Anie Denaud

As a New York City-based doula, my passion is to provide unwavering emotional, physical, and informational support to growing families. I believe every birthing person deserves to feel safe, respected, and empowered. My role is to help you navigate your options, advocate for your wishes, and build confidence for a positive birth and postpartum experience.

With a calming presence and a deep well of knowledge in comfort measures, I'm here to support not just the birthing parent, but partners and family as well, ensuring everyone feels prepared and included.

My Approach to Care

I am dedicated to providing support that is as unique as you are.

Evidence-Based Guidance

I provide up-to-date information so you can make empowered decisions that feel right for you and your family.

A Calming Presence

My goal is to create a peaceful and reassuring atmosphere, reducing anxiety and fostering a positive environment for birth.

Partner-Inclusive Support

I work with your partner as a team, enhancing their role and ensuring they feel confident and involved every step of the way.

My Services

Comprehensive support tailored to your unique needs.

Prenatal Support

3 prenatal visits to discuss your birth preferences, practice comfort techniques, and ensure you and your partner feel fully prepared.

Labor & Birth Support

Continuous in-person support from the moment you need me in active labor, providing comfort, advocacy, and guidance.

Postpartum Support

2-3 post partum visits to process your birth, offer feeding support, and answer newborn care questions for a smooth transition.

Placenta Encapsulation

Safe and professional placenta encapsulation services to support your postpartum recovery and well-being.

A doula provides continuous physical, emotional, and informational support before, during, and after childbirth. We are non-medical professionals who help families have a safe, memorable, and empowered birthing experience. This can include suggesting comfort measures, helping you understand your options, and providing support to your partner.
