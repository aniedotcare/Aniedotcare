Hi, I’m Anie Denaud

As a Brooklyn-based doula, my passion is to provide unwavering emotional, physical, and informational support to growing families. I believe every birthing person deserves to feel safe, respected, and empowered. My role is to help you navigate your options, advocate for your wishes, and build confidence for a positive birth and postpartum experience.

With a calming presence and a deep well of knowledge in comfort measures, I'm here to support not just the birthing parent, but partners and family as well, ensuring everyone feels prepared and included.

My Services

Comprehensive support tailored to your unique needs.

Prenatal Support

Two prenatal visits to discuss your birth preferences, practice comfort techniques, and ensure you and your partner feel fully prepared.

Labor & Birth Support

Continuous in-person support from the moment you need me in active labor, providing comfort, advocacy, and guidance at your home, birth center, or hospital.

Postpartum Support

One postpartum visit to process your birth, offer feeding support, answer newborn care questions, and ensure your family is adjusting smoothly.

We are non-medical professionals who help families have a safe, memorable, and empowered birthing experience.
