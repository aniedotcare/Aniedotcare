## 2025-04-09 - Accessible Mobile Menu Toggles\n**Learning:** Icon-only mobile menu toggle buttons lack context for screen reader users and fail to communicate their state without explicit ARIA attributes.\n**Action:** Added `aria-label="Toggle navigation menu"`, `aria-controls="mobile-menu"`, and dynamically updated `aria-expanded` attributes to ensure the menu's purpose and open/closed state are clearly conveyed to assistive technologies.
